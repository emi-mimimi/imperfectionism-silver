      Quick answers

      Won't hot silver burn a rubber mold?

      Silver goes into a plaster mold that is fired in a kiln and broken off afterwards. Rubber molds are only for making wax copies.

      Can a microwave kiln do the job?

      No. The chamber is about 8 × 4.5 cm, it tops out near 900°C and has no temperature control. A flask won't fit, and sterling needs to be poured hotter than that.

      Do I need a melting furnace?

      No. A gas torch melts small amounts: a butane torch handles about 10–15 g, a gasoline torch with blow pipe more. A furnace only helps with bigger, repeat melts.

      Will casting shops take tiny orders?

      Some do. Many add a few customer waxes to the tree they're already casting that day. Ask about minimum order, price per gram, metal loss and mold fee.
